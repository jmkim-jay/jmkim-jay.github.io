Wireless networks & intelligent control

Jaemin Kim.

Researching how wireless networks learn, adapt, and allocate resources.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at Chung-Ang University, advised by Prof. Sungrae Cho at UCLab.

My research focuses on real-time control and resource allocation in O-RAN, with an emphasis on online learning and optimization. I am also interested in federated learning over wireless networks and digital twin-assisted network control.

In November–December 2026, I am visiting Northeastern University to collaborate with Prof. Tommaso Melodia and WiNEs Lab.
